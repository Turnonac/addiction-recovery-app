What Light2Life is#

Light2Life is a small daily habit for recovery. Each day you name your lowest color, call one person you trust, and they pray for you. Honest, simple, one day at a time.

There’s no account and nothing to sign up for. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It has three tabs: Today, History and Settings.

The four colors always mean the same thing:

  • Green: recovery-oriented actions
  • Blue: careless about avoiding Red
  • Yellow: actions that move toward Red
  • Red: viewed anything explicit

Your day’s color is your lowest moment of the day. The big number on Today is the number of days since your last Red.

Getting started#

How does setup work?#

The first time you open the app, a short setup walks you through six steps:

  1. Welcome.
  2. How the colors work.
  3. When your counter starts: Start counting today, or My last Red day was… and pick the date.
  4. Who you’ll call each day. You can choose from contacts, type a name and number, or tap Skip for now.
  5. What each color looks like for you. Add your own lines, or tap Write these later.
  6. A daily reminder. Pick a time and tap Remind me at…, or choose No reminders for now.

Can I change my counter start date later?#

No. The start date is set only during setup. The counter always counts from your start date or your most recent Red day, whichever is later. So a Red you log for a past day in History restarts the counter from that day only if it’s later than both.

Daily check-ins#

How do I check in?#

On Today, tap Check in (or Update if you already logged today). Pick your lowest color for the day, then tap Save check-in.

I picked the wrong color. Can I fix it?#

Yes. Tap Update and pick again. A color closer to Red replaces the saved one right away. If you pick a better color than the one saved, the app asks you to confirm, in case it was a mistake. A logged day can be changed, but it can’t be set back to “not logged.”

I missed a day. What do I do?#

If yesterday is missing, Today shows Log yesterday. For any other past day, go to History, tap the day, and tap Log it. Days you haven’t logged never reset your counter. Future days stay locked until they start.

What happens when I log Red?#

Your counter restarts at 0. Your history and your best streak stay. If you have a partner, the button becomes Save, then call… and the call screen opens after you save.

How do I edit what each color looks like for me?#

Go to Settings › My colors. Tap the + chip to add a line, or tap a line to edit it or Delete it. Deleting doesn’t ask first. Each line can be up to 120 characters. Changing your list doesn’t change past days.

Your partner and calls#

Does Light2Life need access to my contacts?#

No. Choose from contacts opens your phone’s own contact picker, and only the person you pick is passed to the app. The app saves one name and one phone number. On iPhone, if the contact has several numbers, the app asks which one to use. You can also just type the name and number.

How do I add, change or remove my partner?#

Go to Settings. Tap Add your partner, or tap your partner’s row to edit it. Choose from contacts or type a name and number, then tap Save partner. To remove them, tap Remove partner. Your history and past check-ins stay.

The app doesn’t look at your contacts again after you pick someone. If their number changes, update it here.

What happens on the call screen?#

From Today, tap Call on your daily call card. The call screen shows each day since your last call and your lowest color. On the call, you share your lowest color since your last call, and your partner prays for you. Tap Call to open your phone’s dialer. On iPhone, your phone asks you to confirm the call first.

How do I record that we talked?#

When you come back to the app after calling, it asks Did you talk with…? Tap Yes, we talked. You can also tap I talked with… — mark today on the call screen. For a past day, go to History, tap the day, and turn on Checked in with…. A call that starts before midnight counts for the day it started.

I logged a lower color after we talked. What happens?#

Your check-in stays. You call once a day, and that call still counts. The day goes on your next call under Also share. If you haven’t talked yet today, the app offers to call now. If you already talked today, it waits for tomorrow’s call.

The call goes to the wrong number.#

Light2Life dials exactly the number you saved. It doesn’t add a country code or change the format. Fix it in Settings: tap your partner’s row, edit the number, and save. Include the country code if your phone needs it.

The call won’t start.#

If you see “Couldn’t start the call,” your device couldn’t open a phone dialer. This happens on devices that can’t make phone calls, like some tablets. The message shows your partner’s number so you can call another way. If you see “No number for…,” tap Add number.

Reminders#

How do I turn the reminder on or change the time?#

Go to Settings › Daily reminder. Turn on Remind me to check in, then tap Time. It starts at 9:00 PM. A preview shows exactly what the reminder says. Turn the switch off to stop it.

My reminder isn’t showing up.#

  • Check that Remind me to check in is on in Settings.
  • If you see Open phone settings, notifications are off for Light2Life in your phone’s settings. Tap it and turn them on. On Android, also check that the “Daily reminder” notification category is on.
  • If you see Allow notifications, tap it. This can happen after moving to a new phone.
  • The reminder doesn’t appear while the app is open.
  • On Android, it can arrive a few minutes after the set time.

Why is the reminder so vague?#

On purpose. It says only “Daily check-in” and “Time for your daily check-in.” It never says what the app is for. Your phone still shows the Light2Life name and icon with it.

Backups and new phones#

Your data lives only on your phone. We don’t have a copy. A backup file is the surest way to keep it safe.

How do I back up?#

  1. Go to Settings › Your data › Export backup file.
  2. Your phone’s share sheet opens. Save the file somewhere safe, like Files, a cloud drive or your email.
  3. When the app asks, tap Yes, I saved it.

Settings then shows the date of your last backup. If you haven’t backed up in 30 days, Today shows a “Back up your history” card.

The file is named like daily-backup-2026-10-30.json. It’s plain text that anyone who opens it can read, and it includes your partner’s name and number. It has no password, so keep it somewhere private.

How do I move to a new phone?#

  1. On your old phone, export a backup file and save it somewhere your new phone can reach, like a cloud drive or your email.
  2. On your new phone, install Light2Life and finish the short setup. You can skip the partner, tap Write these later, and choose No reminders for now. Settings opens only after setup.
  3. Go to Settings › Your data › Import backup file, pick the file, and tap Replace.
  4. If your reminder was on, allow notifications when asked.

An iCloud or Google restore of your whole phone may also bring your data over. That depends on your phone and your backup settings.

How does importing work?#

Settings › Your data › Import backup file lets you pick a Light2Life backup file (5 MB or smaller). You’ll see a preview first: how many days it holds and your partner’s name. Tapping Replace replaces everything on this phone with the backup. Your original file isn’t changed.

I deleted the app or lost my phone. Can you get my data back?#

We’re sorry. We never have a copy of your data, so we can’t recover it. It can come back only from a backup file you exported or from your phone’s iCloud or Google backup.

Does iCloud or Google backup include Light2Life?#

If iCloud Backup or Google backup is on in your phone’s settings, your phone may include Light2Life’s data. Whether a restore brings it back depends on your phone and your settings, which the app can’t control. A backup file is the surest option.

The privacy cover#

Why is the app blank or blurred in the app switcher?#

It’s a privacy cover, so people glancing at your phone can’t see your check-ins. On iPhone, the app blurs in the app switcher and whenever it isn’t active, for example behind a system pop-up. On Android, it shows blank in Recent apps. The cover is always on and can’t be turned off.

Why can’t I take screenshots on Android?#

The same privacy cover blocks screenshots of the app on Android. On iPhone, screenshots still work, so take care with any you take.

Deleting your data#

Light2Life doesn’t have a “delete all” button. To delete everything:

  1. Uninstall Light2Life. This removes all of its data from that phone. (On Android, clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings also works.) On Android, if Google backup is on, reinstalling Light2Life can bring your data back. To stop that, turn Google backup off or delete your phone’s Google backup before you reinstall.
  2. Delete any backup files you exported, wherever you saved or sent them.
  3. To remove every copy, delete your phone’s iCloud or Google backups in your phone’s settings.

To remove just some things: remove your partner in Settings, delete a line in Settings › My colors, or turn off a partner check-in by tapping the day in History.

Export a backup file first if you might want your data back. We can’t delete or restore your data for you, because we never have it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Messages you might see#

“Couldn’t open your data”#

Nothing was deleted. Tap Try again, or close Light2Life and open it again. If it keeps happening, restart your phone. Please don’t uninstall the app unless you have a backup file, because uninstalling erases your data.

“Couldn’t save”#

Please try again. If it keeps happening, email us.

“Contacts can’t be opened here”#

Your phone’s contact picker isn’t available. Type the name and number instead. If a contact has no name or no phone number, the app asks you to type it.

“Couldn’t export”#

Sharing isn’t available right now. Please try again in a moment.

Import messages#

  • “This file isn’t a Light2Life backup.” Pick the .json file you exported from Light2Life.
  • “That file couldn’t be opened.” Try again, or download the file again. It must be 5 MB or smaller.
  • “This backup was made by a newer version of Light2Life.” Update the app, then try again.
  • “This backup file is damaged and can’t be used.” Try another copy of the file, if you have one.
  • “Couldn’t import.” Nothing was changed. Please try again.

Reminder messages#

“Reminders are off,” “Couldn’t set the reminder,” “Couldn’t change the reminder” or “Couldn’t change the time”: check that notifications are on for Light2Life in your phone’s settings, then try again in Settings › Daily reminder.

Other questions#

Are there ads or in-app purchases?#

No. Light2Life has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do you collect my data?#

No. There’s no account and no server, and the app doesn’t send what you enter anywhere. Read the Privacy Policy and the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Can I suggest a feature?#

Yes, please. Email us at light2life.help@gmail.com.

Professional and emergency help#

Light2Life is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It isn’t a substitute for a doctor, counselor or recovery program. For medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, please talk with a doctor or another qualified health professional.

If you’re in crisis, or you might hurt yourself or someone else, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency room now. We can’t respond to emergencies by email.
