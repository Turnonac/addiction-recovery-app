Light2Life Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy is for people in Washington State, Nevada and other places with consumer health data laws. “We” means the developer of Light2Life.

The app never sends us your health data, and we don’t sell or share consumer health data. The only way we could receive any is if you choose to write about your health in an email to us.

What stays on your phone
Your daily colors, your partner check-ins and the lines you write under each color. These could say something about your health. They stay on your phone, and we never receive them.
Where it comes from
You. You enter it in the app.
What it’s used for
Only to run the app on your phone, and to go into a backup file if you export one.
Who it’s shared with
No one receives it through the app. If you email us, Google stores the message for us, because our support address is a Gmail account. We have no other third parties or affiliates that receive it.
Tracking
No one tracks you across apps or websites through Light2Life.
Seeing or changing it
You can see and change it in the app at any time, and delete it as described in How to delete your data.
If you email us
If you choose to mention your health in an email, we use it only to reply. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
Changes
We’ll update this page and its date.
Questions or requests
light2life.help@gmail.com
